FAQs

Is the eye mask visible when it's not in use?

No. It tucks away inside the hood, so from the outside it looks like a regular premium hoodie.

Can I remove the eye mask?

No, it's built into the hood on purpose, so there's nothing extra to pack or lose mid-flight.

How does it fit?

Relaxed and a little oversized. Order your usual size for the intended fit, or size down for a closer fit. Our model is 5'6" and wears a Small.

When will it arrive?

FlightMode is made to order, so it's produced after you buy. Delivery is free to Canada and the USA and usually takes about 2–4 weeks, with duties included.

What is your return policy?

You have 14 days from delivery to request a return. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition. See our Refund Policy for details.
