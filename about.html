About LongFlight

The Story Behind LongFlight

LongFlight was born from a simple frustration — long flights and road trips spent uncomfortable, restless, and wishing travel clothes actually felt good to wear. It's built for travelers who value style, quality, and simplicity.

How we work

Three rules, kept.

Fabric that performs

Soft, durable cottons and cotton-polyester blends, chosen so each piece stays comfortable and holds its shape through a full day of transit.

Made for the Journey.

Thoughtful details and comfortable designs made with travel and everyday wear in mind.

Comfort Meets Versatility.

Easy-to-wear pieces designed to feel just as good on the journey as they do wherever the day takes you.

The trip is long. What you wear shouldn't make it feel longer.
